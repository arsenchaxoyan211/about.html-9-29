About Fortnite

Fortnite is a popular online multiplayer game developed by Epic Games. Players can explore, collect items, build, and complete against other players.

Game Modes

  • Battle Royale
  • Creative
  • Zero Build
  • Save the World

Gameplay

Players can collect weapons and items, explore the map, build structures, and try to be the last player or team standing.

Features

Fortnite has different maps, characters, vehicles, events.
