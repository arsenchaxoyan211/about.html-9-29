About the Game

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is an action-adventure game developed by Insomniac Games for PlayStaion 5.

Characters

  • Peter Parker
  • Miles Morales
  • Venom
  • Kraven The Hunter

Gameplay

Players can explore the New York City, swing through the city, fight enemies, complete missions, and use different abilities.

Features

The game lets players switch between Peter Parker and Miles Morales and explore a larger version of New York City.
