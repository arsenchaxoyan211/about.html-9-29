About GTA 5

Grand Theft Auto V is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar Games. The game takes place in Los Santos.

Main Characters

  • Michael De Santa
  • Franklin Clinton
  • Trevor Philips

What Can You Do?

Players can explore the city, drive cars, complete missions, fly planes, ride motorcycles, and explore the open-world.

Game Modes

  • Story Mode
  • GTA online

Location

The game takes place in the fictional city of Los Santos and the surrounding areas.
