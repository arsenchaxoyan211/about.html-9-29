About Rocket League

Rocket League is a sports game that combines soccer with fast cars. Players use cars to hit a giant ball and score goals.

Gameplay

  • Drive cars
  • Hit the ball
  • Score goals
  • Play with friends

Game Modes

  • 1v1
  • 2v2
  • 3v3
  • 4v4

Features

Players can customize their cars, play online matches, perform jumps and aerial moves, and complete with teams.
